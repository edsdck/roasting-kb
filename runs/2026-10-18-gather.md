## Suggestions for gaps.md
- Gap #1: S0055 and S0057 are opposite poles: forum experts put the safe baseline at 2:00-2:15 and 15-20 F of development, while a 2026 Australian light-roast recipe proposes 1:00-1:20 after first crack (and a very small temperature rise). Neither has scores or replicates; still no controlled sensory study below 1:30. S0057's "degrees" are read on the roaster's own probe, so they do not transfer to a Skywalker probe.
- Gap #2: nothing IR-specific again. S0061 offers a competing explanation worth testing: immature beans / quakers are the main source of astringency, so sort the roasted beans before blaming the profile.
- Gap #3: still no 350 g Skywalker profile with cup notes.
- Gap #4: S0054 adds a measured probe effect (RTD vs K-type, 365 vs 385 F first crack) next to S0045.
- Gap #5: S0059 and S0062 give two independent loss-to-roast-level tables that roughly agree (City+ 13.3 %, light 11-13 %); both warn that green moisture shifts the meaning of a given loss.
- Gap #6: the Barista Hustle teaser (rejected row) says radiation is negligible in most roasters per Schenker and Rothgeb 2017, while S0033 puts radiation first. S0054 relays a 90 / 7 / 3.5 % convection / radiation / conduction split for a 250 lb drum from Schwartzberg and Adluri (2007). Both source papers should be found; renatoa's post 80382 still not located.
- Gap #7: S0060 (definitions), S0051 (baked vs underdeveloped profiles, loss alone did not separate them) and S0055.
- Gap #9: S0058 (Hoos: most people run airflow too low) now sits against S0040/S0041 (restricted airflow fixed a green natural Rwanda); S0047, S0052 and S0053 add three further tests where lower or higher airflow changed the cup only slightly or in different directions. Evidence is mixed and none of it is on a lamp roaster.
- Gap #11 / #8: S0056 is the strongest evidence that matching total time, first-crack time, development and end colour makes roasters indistinguishable to expert tasters, including a 2 kg Diedrich IR-5.
- Gap #12: S0048 and S0049 now cover storage after opening (original bag with closure beat a transferred canister; oxygen is the main driver). No taste data for home-roasted beans.
- Gap #14: S0050 gives volatile groups for under-/over-developed roasts, with no times or sensory data.
