# Gaps — what the Gatherer should look for next

*Maintained by the Builder. Most important first. Updated 2026-10-16 (Build 5).*

1. **Very short development — controlled sensory evidence below 1:30, and the "degrees above first
   crack" idea** (owner sits at ~1:00, DTR 7–12 %, median +8.3 °C above FC; the cupped roast ended
   +6.2 °C). Now have a blind-test *claim* that a vegetal note vanishes ~10 °F above FC regardless of
   time (S0023), Hoos at 1:00–1:10 for a natural (S0025), a V2 owner at 85–100 s (S0027), but no
   data. Angles: N3Roaster's roastingbook.coffee material; Home-Barista "Anthony Douglas WBC Roast
   Profile" (2022, origin of the 10 °F remark); SCA / Coffee Science Foundation / CoffeeMind studies
   on light-roast development; Nordic Roasters Forum talks; the Robusta under/over-development
   paper (needs-manual: owner could save the PDF from IJASEIT).
2. **IR-specific astringency, tipping and "done" flavour** (new). S0026 gives one Kaleido
   user's recipe (higher charge, lower power %, tiny stepwise cuts); nothing yet from Skywalker
   owners with cup notes. Angles: Home-Barista "Roasting on a Kaleido" (2026-01), "Profile tips"
   (M10), Kaleido Sniper thread (13+ pages), kaleidoroasters.com manual-mode article; Hoos
   "Tipping and its Avoidance" and the Keys to the Shop RoR #18 interview; Skywalker Discord
   summaries.
3. **Skywalker at 350 g with cupping notes** — still missing a V1 350 g profile with cup notes
   (S0027 has V1 350–400 g impressions only; recipes are 250 g). Angles: Home Roasters threads 7105
   "Roast Assistant for Skywalker" and 7103; HiBean community light-roast profiles; Nirecue videos;
   TomPoland's V1 review video (CoffeeSnobs post #8); Roastetta Skywalker V1 entries with notes.
4. **Why first crack reads higher for some coffees** — still contested (S0020, S0024). Need:
   moisture/density/processing vs FC reading at matched profile speed. Angles: Home-Barista "No
   audible sound of first crack" (2026-07) and "Add moisture during a roast?" (2025-10); JAFC 2008
   Baggenstoss et al. full text; acoustic FC studies.
5. **Green moisture and weight loss** (new, from S0025): Hoos targets light-roast loss ~1–2 % above
   green moisture; owner's median loss is 13.4 % with unknown moisture. Angles: home moisture
   meters (Lighttells/DiFluid-type), moisture vs roast loss data, supplier moisture figures;
   cultivar groups and roast speed (Hoos's *Cultivar* book, other cultivar roast studies).
6. **Flat vs declining RoR on IR** — renatoa contradicts his own earlier advice (S0015 vs S0017).
   Find his radiant-vs-convection post (homeroasters post 80382) and any IR logs paired with taste
   (Kaleido, Rubasse, DigiRosto users).
7. **What causes "baked"** — practitioner views only. Controlled sensory work at equal colour
   (crash vs no crash; long dev + flat RoR; dev out of proportion to total time).
8. **Fast IR roasts: dark outside, under-developed inside?** (S0015, S0017, S0026, S0027) — any
   whole-bean vs ground colour or surface/core measurements under radiant heating; NIR vs FIR
   penetration into beans. (Only a fluid-bed response-time measurement exists, S0023.)
9. **Airflow on the Skywalker and Hoos's airflow results** — Hoos says airflow changes taste and
   weight-loss curve (S0025) without data; need his data when published, plus how big a fan step
   changes RoR on a lamp roaster and fan curves at 350 g (S0015, S0027 only).
10. **Home cupping & roast degree** — simple protocol, cheap colour measurement (phone/Lighttells/
    DiFluid), weight loss as proxy; triangle testing at home.
11. **Pre-first-crack phases / browning length** — S0026 (IKAWA stepped profiles) links a longer
    browning phase to less vegetal/astringent character; S0011/S0012 tension remains. Sensory work
    at equal colour and development.
12. **Resting / degassing** — filter vs espresso; one owner says Skywalker roasts degas more slowly
    (not yet extracted).
13. **Roast chemistry basics** — Maillard, caramelization, acids, what changes when; the vegetal/raw
    compound (hexanal suggested in S0023, untested) and when it leaves the bean (review papers).
14. **Artisan settings for the Skywalker** — delta span, smoothing, spike filtering for the noisy
    NTC stream and trier dips (S0017); PID versus event-replay on IR roasters (S0026, S0009).

Owner-side questions (not for the Gatherer): cup id39 and id40 blind side by side (same coffee,
+6.2 vs +12.0 °C above FC); what is the green moisture of the coffees you roast; does your V1
auto-profile table match S0016; how do you mark first crack (first pop vs regular pops); room
temperature when roasting.
