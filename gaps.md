# Gaps — what the Gatherer should look for next

*Maintained by the Builder. Most important first. Updated 2026-10-16 (Build 3).*

1. **Skywalker V1 at 350 g — real profiles** — preheat/charge, heater/fan schedules, phase times and
   first-crack readings from experienced owners, ideally with cupping notes. Angles: nirecue
   YouTube (search in the browser, use transcripts), Skywalker Discord summaries, Home Roaster Forum
   (Renatoa), roastetta.com shared HiBean profiles, Home-Barista.
2. **Very short development (~1:00, DTR 7–12 %)** — any sensory evidence below 1:30 development;
   what light-roast specialists target on small roasters. (Owner's roasts sit here.)
3. **Why first crack reads higher for some coffees** (honey ~190 °C vs washed ~184 °C on the same
   machine) — processing, density, moisture.
4. **Flat vs declining RoR on IR roasters** — any IR logs paired with taste; still reasoning-only
   ([contested](docs/kb/myths-and-contested.md)).
5. **What causes "baked"** — controlled sensory work (crash vs no crash, long dev + flat RoR) at equal
   colour; currently only practitioner views (S0008, S0010).
6. **Surface defects under radiant heat** — scorching/tipping risk with IR lamps; how to spot and
   avoid on small electric roasters.
7. **Home cupping & roast degree** — simple protocol, cheap colour measurement, weight loss as proxy.
8. **Resting / degassing** — filter vs espresso timelines.
9. **Green coffee moisture & processing** — washed/natural/honey and moisture effects on profiles.
10. **Pre-first-crack phases** — sensory evidence on drying/Maillard duration at equal colour and
   development (resolve S0011 vs S0012 tension).
11. **Roast chemistry basics** — Maillard, caramelization, acids, what changes when (good review
   papers).
12. **Artisan settings for slow/noisy probes** — delta span, smoothing, NTC probe lag.
