# Gaps — what the Gatherer should look for next

*Maintained by the Builder. Roughly most important first; numbers are kept stable between builds so
source files can refer to them. Updated 2026-10-18 (Build 7).*

1. **Very short development — controlled sensory evidence below 1:30, and the "degrees above first
   crack" idea** (owner sits at ~1:00, DTR 7–12 %, median +8.3 °C above FC; the cupped roast ended
   +6.2 °C). The field now splits: a 2026 blind-tasting report and chart say 1:00–1:20 (S0057,
   S0063, unscored), a gas-drum beginner thread says 2:00–2:15 and 8.3–11.1 °C (S0055, many
   confounders, no cup test of any fix). Nearest controlled work: a Gayo study at 2:50–4:50 (S0037,
   colour confounded) and Sweet Maria's Probat series (S0034, shortest crack-to-drop 1:32). Angles:
   scores or taster counts behind the Roast Summit tastings; N3Roaster's roastingbook.coffee
   material; Home-Barista "Anthony Douglas WBC Roast Profile" (2022); SCA / Coffee Science
   Foundation / CoffeeMind studies; Nordic Roasters Forum talks; Hacea's fixed-385 °F marking; the
   Robusta under/over-development paper and the Wójcicki PDF (both needs-manual: owner could save
   the PDFs).
2. **IR-specific astringency, tipping and "done" flavour.** S0026 is one Kaleido user's recipe;
   S0041 walks an astringency fix through on a gas drum; S0029 gives only power steps. New
   competing explanation: immature beans and quakers (S0061, a relayed study, not read). Nothing
   from Skywalker owners with cup notes. Angles: Rabelo et al. 2021 (quakers) itself; Home-Barista
   "Roasting on a Kaleido" (2026-01), Kaleido Sniper thread, kaleidoroasters.com manual-mode article;
   Hoos "Tipping and its Avoidance"; Keys to the Shop RoR #18 and the Springer far-infrared paper
   (both needs-manual); Skywalker Discord summaries.
3. **Skywalker at 350 g with cupping notes** — still no V1 350 g profile with cup notes (S0027 has impressions only; recipes are 250 g). Angles: Home Roasters threads 7105
   and 7103; HiBean community light-roast profiles; Nirecue videos; TomPoland's V1 review video;
   Roastetta Skywalker V1 entries with notes.
4. **Why first crack reads different on one machine** — probe depth moved one Quest by ~11 °C
   (S0045), probe type moved a gas drum by ~11 °C (S0054), a stray pop can lead the main crack by
   5.6–8.3 °C (S0055), airflow may shift the probe (S0053); a dehydrated-green test still cracked at
   the same temperature (S0031); Bullets are reported at ~182, ~200 and ~173 °C (S0039, S0020,
   S0047). Need: moisture/density/processing vs FC reading at matched speed, fixed probe and fixed
   call, ideally on a Skywalker. Angles: Home-Barista "No audible sound of first crack" (2026-07);
   JAFC 2008 Baggenstoss et al. full text; acoustic FC studies.
5. **Green moisture and weight loss** — S0030–S0032 give typical ranges (11–15 % loss, ~10 % very
   light); S0059 and S0062 add two practitioner bands that agree (light 11–13 %, City+ ~13 %);
   S0040 (15 % loss, green cup) and S0051 (baked 14.8 %, balanced 15.0 %, grassy 13.0 %) show loss
   alone misleads. Need: a home moisture-meter comparison, moisture vs loss at equal colour, how
   loss relates to Agtron (Hoos's readers asked, unanswered), chaff's share of the loss, cultivar
   groups and roast speed (Hoos's *Cultivar*).
6. **Flat vs declining RoR on IR, with tasting** — renatoa contradicts his own earlier advice (S0015
   vs S0017); two C-grade forum threads (S0044, S0046) argue the physics but give no cup data. Find
   renatoa's radiant-vs-convection post (homeroasters post 80382), a measured declining-vs-flat
   comparison with tasting, and any IR logs paired with taste (Kaleido, Rubasse, DigiRosto users).
7. **What causes "baked", and what it means** — practitioner views only, now four definitions
   (RoR crash; long development at equal colour; Perger's "lack of sweetness in a coffee that isn't
   dark", second-hand; and Hoos's refusal to use the word, S0060) and a seventh proposed cause
   (long low-temperature roasting or heat/airflow out of balance, S0051). Controlled sensory work
   at equal colour (crash vs no crash; long dev + flat RoR; stall in first crack, S0034). Perger's
   Barista Hustle article is gone (needs a saved copy). Hoos's other defect posts (e.g. "Exhaust Air
   Temperature") not yet read.
8. **Fast IR roasts: dark outside, under-developed inside?** (S0015, S0017, S0026, S0027). S0033's
   Biot numbers (0.48–0.82) fit it for a gas drum, but need whole-bean vs ground colour or
   surface/core measurements under radiant heating; NIR vs FIR penetration. The matched-profile
   pilot (S0056) ends colour within 3 Agtron points on whole and ground; a lamp roaster is not in it.
9. **Airflow on the Skywalker** — restricted-vs-high airflow is a live dispute across machines
   (S0040/S0041, S0052, S0053 for lower; S0047, S0058, S0026/S0027 for higher; S0054: little change
   when ET is held). Every test is one pair on a drum, hot-air or fluid-bed machine; none is on a
   lamp roaster, and each changed heat or inlet temperature with the air. Need: how big a fan step
   changes RoR, time and weight loss on a lamp roaster, fan curves at 350 g, and Hoos's unpublished
   airflow data (S0025); Hoos's "LeBrew Color Meter" and "Exhaust Air Temperature" posts; a method
   for reading an exhaust-to-bean gap without a drum exhaust probe (S0058).
10. **Charge temperature for light roasts and lamp roasters** — the only controlled test (S0043) is
    medium colour on a 5 kg production roaster; S0055 and S0026 say a cool charge leaves the inside
    behind. Need light-roast charge tests, ideally with first-crack time and weight loss reported.
11. **Pre-first-crack phases / browning length** — S0035 (±35 s of drying audible), S0026 (longer
    browning, less vegetal), S0039/S0038 (Hacea phases), S0063 (chart: 4:00 → 3:30 Maillard means
    less sweetness and body, no data) and S0057 (faster roasts preferred in a time set) against
    S0011/S0012. Need equal-colour, equal-development sensory work; Sweet Maria's "Stretchin' Out"
    part 1.
12. **Resting, degassing and storage** — S0042 measures gas loss (light roasts degas least), S0036
    collects rest-time opinion (1 to 6 weeks), S0048 measures storage after opening by chemistry
    (screw-cap bag best, transferred canister worst) and S0049 reviews staling (oxygen, early loss).
    No taste-based resting or storage data; nothing on humidity, freezing or portioning. One owner
    says Skywalker roasts degas more slowly (not yet extracted). Angles: the Sivetz staling blog
    (needs-manual); a sensory follow-up to Smrke 2022; Hoos/Hacea on rest.
13. **Home cupping & roast degree** — simple protocol, cheap colour measurement (phone/Lighttells/
    DiFluid), weight loss as proxy; triangle testing at home (the 2019 pilot, S0056, is the only
    triangle data and is for professionals).
14. **Roast chemistry basics** — Maillard, caramelization, acids; two volatile-group studies now
    exist (S0050: start temperature 210/220/240 °C on 5 kg; S0064: three IKAWA roast levels on 46
    lots) but neither gives times, colour or sensory scores. The raw-note compound (hexanal, suggested
    in S0023) is higher in washed green coffee (S0064) but its link to a grassy roasted cup is
    untested; the "6–7 % moisture needed for Maillard" claim (S0039) has no source behind it.
15. **Artisan settings for the Skywalker** — delta span, smoothing, spike filtering for the noisy NTC
    stream (S0017, S0046); MaKoMo's idle-noise blog is the lead; PID versus event-replay on IR.
16. **Radiation, convection and conduction shares** (new) — S0033 puts radiation first in a fitted
    gas-drum model; S0054 relays 90 / 7 / 3.5 % (convection / radiation / conduction) for a 250 lb
    drum from Schwartzberg and Adluri (Roast Magazine, 2007), which was not found; a Barista Hustle
    teaser cites Schenker and Rothgeb (2017) for negligible radiation, also not found. Angles: Roast
    Magazine archive for the 2007 article, the Schenker thesis (ETH Zurich) and the 2017 paper.
17. **Do matched profiles stay indistinguishable on a small lamp roaster?** (new) — S0056 is a pilot
    on 2–7 kg drums with no significance test and an unnamed coffee; S0052 reports real differences
    between a Diedrich and a Loring. Need a replication or any matched-profile test that includes a
    small radiant or hot-air roaster, and the Cooper/Hoos follow-up material.

Owner-side questions (not for the Gatherer): cup id39 and id40 blind side by side at the same rest
(same coffee, +6.2 vs +12.0 °C above FC); what is the green moisture of the coffees you roast; how deep
is your probe and does the tip stay covered at 350 g; how many days do you rest before tasting; does
your V1 auto-profile table match S0016; how do you mark first crack (first pop vs regular pops) — a
stray pop can lead the main crack by 5.6–8.3 °C (S0055); what fan setting do you run through the roast
and does it change by season; how do you store roasted coffee between roasting and drinking (container,
days); room temperature when roasting; do you have a saved copy of Perger's "Let's talk about roasting".
