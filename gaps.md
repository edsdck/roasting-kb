   7–12 %). Now have practitioner views on both sides (S0018 vs S0020–S0022) but no tasting study
   below 1:30. Angles: SCA/CoffeeMind/Coffee Science Foundation studies on light-roast development,

   (JAFC 2008 Baggenstoss et al. full text); acoustic FC studies.

5. **What causes "baked"** — controlled sensory work at equal colour (crash vs no crash; long dev +

6. **Fast IR roasts: dark outside, under-developed inside?** (S0015, S0017) — any measurements of
   whole-bean vs ground colour, or surface/core temperature, under radiant heating; NIR vs FIR

7. **Home cupping & roast degree** — simple protocol, cheap colour measurement (phone/Lighttells/
   DiFluid), weight loss as proxy; triangle testing at home.
8. **Airflow on the Skywalker** — how big a fan step changes RoR; fan curves at 350 g; ambient

9. **Resting / degassing** — filter vs espresso; one owner says Skywalker roasts degas more slowly

10. **Green coffee moisture & processing** — washed/natural/honey/decaf roasting behaviour beyond

11. **Pre-first-crack phases** — sensory evidence on drying/Maillard duration at equal colour and
    development (S0011/S0012 tension; S0020–S0022 lean toward "late Maillard matters").
12. **Roast chemistry basics** — Maillard, caramelization, acids, what changes when (review papers).
13. **Artisan settings for the Skywalker** — delta span, smoothing, spike filtering for the noisy
