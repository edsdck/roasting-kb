# Gaps — what the Gatherer should look for next

*Maintained by the Builder. Most important first. Updated 2026-10-17 (Build 6).*

1. **Very short development — controlled sensory evidence below 1:30, and the "degrees above first
   crack" idea** (owner sits at ~1:00, DTR 7–12 %, median +8.3 °C above FC; the cupped roast ended
   +6.2 °C). Nearest now: a Gayo study at 2:50–4:50 (S0037, colour confounded) and Sweet Maria's
   Probat series (S0034, shortest crack-to-drop 1:32). Still only claims below that: N3Roaster's
   vegetal note vanishing ~10 °F above FC (S0023), Hoos at 1:00–1:10 (S0025), a V2 owner at 85–100 s
   (S0027). Angles: N3Roaster's roastingbook.coffee material; Home-Barista "Anthony Douglas WBC Roast
   Profile" (2022); SCA / Coffee Science Foundation / CoffeeMind studies; Nordic Roasters Forum
   talks; Hacea's fixed-385 °F marking and their other profile posts; the Robusta
   under/over-development paper (needs-manual: owner could save the PDF from IJASEIT).
2. **IR-specific astringency, tipping and "done" flavour.** S0026 is one Kaleido user's recipe;
   S0041 is the first article that walks an astringency fix through with cup notes, but on a gas
   drum (soak + restricted air); S0029 gives only power steps. Nothing from Skywalker owners with cup
   notes. Angles: Home-Barista "Roasting on a Kaleido" (2026-01), Kaleido Sniper thread,
   kaleidoroasters.com manual-mode article; Hoos "Tipping and its Avoidance"; Keys to the Shop RoR #18
   and the Springer far-infrared paper (both needs-manual); Skywalker Discord summaries.
3. **Skywalker at 350 g with cupping notes** — still no V1 350 g profile with cup notes after three
   more rounds (S0027 has impressions only; recipes are 250 g). Angles: Home Roasters threads 7105 and
   7103; HiBean community light-roast profiles; Nirecue videos; TomPoland's V1 review video;
   Roastetta Skywalker V1 entries with notes.
4. **Why first crack reads different on one machine** — probe depth moved one Quest by ~11 °C
   (S0045); a dehydrated-green test still cracked at the same temperature (S0031); Bullets are
   reported at ~182 and ~200 °C (S0039, S0020). Need: moisture/density/processing vs FC reading at
   matched speed and fixed probe depth, ideally on a Skywalker. Angles: Home-Barista "No audible
   sound of first crack" (2026-07); JAFC 2008 Baggenstoss et al. full text; acoustic FC studies.
5. **Green moisture and weight loss** — S0030–S0032 now give typical ranges (11–15 % loss, ~10 % very
   light) and one measured moisture (~9.6 %), and S0040 shows 15 % loss with a green cup, so loss
   alone misleads. Need: a home moisture-meter comparison, moisture vs roast loss at equal colour,
   cultivar groups and roast speed (Hoos's *Cultivar*).
6. **Flat vs declining RoR on IR, with tasting** — renatoa contradicts his own earlier advice (S0015
   vs S0017); two C-grade forum threads (S0044, S0046) argue the physics but give no cup data. Find
   renatoa's radiant-vs-convection post (homeroasters post 80382), a measured declining-vs-flat
   comparison with tasting, and any IR logs paired with taste (Kaleido, Rubasse, DigiRosto users).
7. **What causes "baked", and what it means** — practitioner views only, and three definitions now
   (RoR crash; long development at equal colour; Perger's "lack of sweetness in a coffee that isn't
   dark", second-hand). Controlled sensory work at equal colour (crash vs no crash; long dev + flat
   RoR; stall in first crack, S0034). Perger's Barista Hustle article is gone (needs a saved copy).
8. **Fast IR roasts: dark outside, under-developed inside?** (S0015, S0017, S0026, S0027). S0033's
   Biot numbers (0.48–0.82) fit it for a gas drum, but need whole-bean vs ground colour or
   surface/core measurements under radiant heating; NIR vs FIR penetration.
9. **Airflow on the Skywalker** — restricted-vs-high airflow is now a live dispute (S0040/S0041 vs
   S0026/S0027); Hoos's airflow data (S0025) still unpublished; need how big a fan step changes RoR
   and weight loss on a lamp roaster, and fan curves at 350 g.
10. **Charge temperature for light roasts and lamp roasters** (new) — the only controlled test
    (S0043) is medium colour on a 5 kg production roaster; need light-roast charge tests, ideally with
    first-crack time and weight loss reported.
11. **Pre-first-crack phases / browning length** — S0035 (±35 s of drying audible), S0026 (longer
    browning vs less vegetal), S0039/S0038 (Hacea phases) against S0011/S0012. Need equal-colour,
    equal-development sensory work; Sweet Maria's "Stretchin' Out" part 1.
12. **Resting, degassing and storage** — S0042 measures gas loss (light roasts degas least), S0036
    collects rest-time opinion (1 to 6 weeks); no taste-based resting data, and nothing yet on
    storage or staling. One owner says Skywalker roasts degas more slowly (not yet extracted).
13. **Home cupping & roast degree** — simple protocol, cheap colour measurement (phone/Lighttells/
    DiFluid), weight loss as proxy; triangle testing at home.
14. **Roast chemistry basics** — Maillard, caramelization, acids; the vegetal/raw compound (hexanal
    suggested in S0023, untested); the "6–7 % moisture needed for Maillard" claim (S0039) has no
    source behind it.
15. **Artisan settings for the Skywalker** — delta span, smoothing, spike filtering for the noisy NTC
    stream (S0017, S0046); MaKoMo's idle-noise blog is the lead; PID versus event-replay on IR.

Owner-side questions (not for the Gatherer): cup id39 and id40 blind side by side at the same rest
(same coffee, +6.2 vs +12.0 °C above FC); what is the green moisture of the coffees you roast; how deep
is your probe and does the tip stay covered at 350 g; how many days do you rest before tasting; does
your V1 auto-profile table match S0016; how do you mark first crack (first pop vs regular pops);
room temperature when roasting; do you have a saved copy of Perger's "Let's talk about roasting".
