# Glossary

**Agtron** — colour scale for roasted coffee (higher = lighter). Agtron 76 is a light roast. [S0006](../sources/S0006-alstrup-2020-development-time-sensory.md)

**BT / ET** — bean temperature (probe in the bean pile) / environment temperature (air in the drum). On the Skywalker BT reads an air–bean mix. [S0003](../sources/S0003-skywiki-renatoa-words-of-wisdom.md)

**Charge** — loading green beans into the preheated roaster; time zero.

**Crash** — sudden drop in RoR, typically around first crack, often linked to moisture release. [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md)

**Delta span** — Artisan's window (seconds) for computing RoR. Shorter = more responsive, noisier. [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md)

**Development time / DTR** — time from first crack to drop / that time as % of total roast time. [S0006](../sources/S0006-alstrup-2020-development-time-sensory.md)

**First crack (FC)** — audible cracking as steam and gases break the bean structure; reads ~182 °C on a Skywalker [S0001](../sources/S0001-skywiki-infrared-roaster.md).

**Flick** — late rise of RoR after a crash or near the drop. [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md)

**IR / FIR** — infrared / far-infrared radiant heating. [S0004](../sources/S0004-puntuwanit-2023-far-infrared-power.md)

**Rate of rise (RoR)** — °/min rise of bean temperature. [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md)

**Titratable acidity (TA)** — amount of acid in brewed coffee measured by titration; correlates with perceived sourness. [S0007](../sources/S0007-anokye-bempah-2024-titratable-acidity.md)

**Turning point (TP)** — lowest BT reading after charge, where the probe starts tracking the beans' rise.

**Weight loss** — (green − roasted) ÷ green; a practical proxy for roast degree.
