# Roast phases

!!! abstract "In short"
    - A roast is usually split into **drying** (charge → yellowing), **browning/Maillard** (yellowing → first crack) and **development** (first crack → drop).
    - At the **same colour**, development time changes flavour more than time to first crack. `conf: medium`
    - Practitioners still stress the early phases: a roast that rushes through drying/browning can taste worse. **Some tension** between sources.
    - Plan with **checkpoints** (time + temperature + RoR at yellow and first crack).

## Concepts
- **Phase timing vs development.** Across eight sensory studies, at equal colour, development time
  affected every attribute measured while time to first crack affected only some; colour (roast
  degree) was the strongest predictor of all. `conf: medium` [S0012](../sources/S0012-munchow-2020-roasting-conditions-multi-study.md) [S0006](../sources/S0006-alstrup-2020-development-time-sensory.md)
- **Early phases matter (practitioner view).** Of two espresso roasts, the one that went fast
  through the first two phases had low body and sweetness and a dry, tart, bitter finish compared
  with a more gradual roast; many roasters "aim for first crack, then think about development".
  `conf: low` `ctx: gas-drum` [S0011](../sources/S0011-cooper-cropster-rate-of-rise-production.md)
- **Matching phases across machines.** Coffees roasted on three machines with different probes
  tasted the same when phase timings (and end colour) matched — curve shapes didn't. `conf: low` [S0011](../sources/S0011-cooper-cropster-rate-of-rise-production.md)
- **What first crack is.** Mainly steam pressure from the bean's ~10–12 % residual moisture (not
  the only driver); the bean swells, the crease opens and chaff is released; the roast turns from
  endothermic to exothermic. Cues besides sound: patchy colour, rounder edges, grain/bread smell.
  `conf: low` [S0019](../sources/S0019-sweet-marias-first-crack-faq.md)
- **Time to first crack has no "correct" value** — it depends on heat-transfer type, batch size, the
  coffee and ambient: ~3–7 min in small air roasters, 10–12 in a Behmor, 9–12 in 5–12 kg shop drums.
  [S0019](../sources/S0019-sweet-marias-first-crack-faq.md)

## In practice
- **Checkpoints (example, not a target):** Cooper's milk-espresso profile on a 10 kg drum: yellow at
  5–6 min, ≥ 4 min yellow → first crack, 3:00–3:30 first crack → drop. [S0011](../sources/S0011-cooper-cropster-rate-of-rise-production.md)
- **Log phase durations** for every roast and compare like with like.
- **Priority order** (from the research): get roast degree right first, then tune development,
  then early phases. **Inference** from [S0012](../sources/S0012-munchow-2020-roasting-conditions-multi-study.md).

## On the Skywalker (IR)
- Your Artisan logs already mark CHARGE, DRY, FCs and DROP, so every roast page shows drying,
  Maillard and development durations. Your medians: drying 4:40, Maillard 4:06, development 1:08
  ([findings](../skywalker/findings.md)).
- Phase *durations* transfer between machines better than temperatures; yellowing at
  "160–165 °C" on a drum won't match the Skywalker's lower-reading probe [S0001](../sources/S0001-skywiki-infrared-roaster.md) [S0009](../sources/S0009-skywiki-putrid-upstairs-response.md).

  in between. Call dry end when the last green/silver hue is gone but before yellow, or by smell

  ~150 °C when first crack is ~181–182 °C. `conf: low` [S0015](../sources/S0015-homeroasters-skywalker-roasts-thread.md) [S0016](../sources/S0016-homeroasters-skywalker-main-thread.md)

## Contested
**How much do pre-first-crack phases matter?** Sensory research: less than development at equal
colour, but not zero [S0012](../sources/S0012-munchow-2020-roasting-conditions-multi-study.md). Practitioner experience: rushing early phases hurts body and
sweetness [S0011](../sources/S0011-cooper-cropster-rate-of-rise-production.md). Not strictly contradictory — the practitioner example may also differ in
development and colour. Other practitioners also put weight on late Maillard (closing the
inner/outer temperature gap there [S0020](../sources/S0020-home-barista-bean-temperature-at-first-crack.md); development "takes place in Maillard, too" [S0021](../sources/S0021-home-barista-development-time-light-roasts.md)), and
one Nordic roaster chose a longer drying phase for a dry, lower-quality Kenya [S0022](../sources/S0022-home-barista-nordic-roasters-forum-exercise.md). Open.
