# Myths & contested questions

Where credible sources disagree. Both sides are shown; nothing here is settled.

| Question | Side A | Side B | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does an RoR crash (or non-declining RoR) cause "baked" coffee? | Yes — main cause of baked coffee is a pronounced RoR crash (Rao) [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md) | "Constantly declining RoR" is a weak, untestable theory; use time, temperature, colour (Münchow) [S0005](../sources/S0005-coffeemind-rate-of-rise-bad-reference.md) | contested — [details](rate-of-rise.md#contested) |
| Is a flat RoR bad? | On convection machines a flat RoR implies rising energy input, can taste baked/dull [S0002](../sources/S0002-skywiki-convection-vs-radiant.md) [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md); one Skywalker owner earlier advised a continuously declining RoR after dry end [S0017](../sources/S0017-homeroasters-skywalker-mods-thread.md) | On a radiant (IR) roaster a flat RoR can just mean stable energy delivery [S0002](../sources/S0002-skywiki-convection-vs-radiant.md); the same owner now calls a flat drying RoR "the new normal" [S0015](../sources/S0015-homeroasters-skywalker-roasts-thread.md) | open for IR — test on the Skywalker |
| How much do phases before first crack matter? | Less than development, at equal colour [S0012](../sources/S0012-munchow-2020-roasting-conditions-multi-study.md) | Rushing early phases hurts body/sweetness [S0011](../sources/S0011-cooper-cropster-rate-of-rise-production.md); a longer browning phase went with less vegetal/astringent character on an IKAWA [S0026](../sources/S0026-home-barista-kaleido-m10-astringent-roasts.md) | open — [Roast phases](roast-phases.md#contested) |
| What causes "baked"? | RoR crash (Rao) [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md) | No consensus: crash, flick, stall, or 4+ min development with flat RoR [S0010](../sources/S0010-hoos-loring-avoiding-roasting-defects.md) | contested — [Defects](defects.md#baked) |
| Is a 20–25 % DTR needed? | Yes — a prerequisite for a successful roast (Rao, 2014; one practitioner's view, grade C) [S0018](../sources/S0018-rao-2014-development-time-ratio.md) | Not for light roasts: 13–16 % / 1:20–1:30 is common [S0021](../sources/S0021-home-barista-development-time-light-roasts.md); Nordic ~45 s [S0022](../sources/S0022-home-barista-nordic-roasters-forum-exercise.md); "not gospel" [S0020](../sources/S0020-home-barista-bean-temperature-at-first-crack.md); Hoos's fruit-forward natural uses 1:00–1:10 [S0025](../sources/S0025-roast-rebels-hoos-cultivars-airflow-interview.md); a V2 owner works at 85–100 s [S0027](../sources/S0027-coffeesnobs-skywalker-roasters-thread.md) | contested — [Development](development.md#contested) |
| End the roast by seconds after first crack or by temperature? | Seconds; FC temperature is just a marker [S0024](../sources/S0024-home-barista-first-crack-temperature-changed.md) | Absolute drop temperature [S0024](../sources/S0024-home-barista-first-crack-temperature-changed.md); a vegetal note that vanishes ~5.6 °C above FC, time-independent [S0023](../sources/S0023-home-barista-vegetal-flavor-first-crack.md) | contested — [Development](development.md#contested) |
| Does first-crack temperature depend on the coffee? | No — same reading for all beans on consistent machines; probe and placement dominate [S0020](../sources/S0020-home-barista-bean-temperature-at-first-crack.md) [S0024](../sources/S0024-home-barista-first-crack-temperature-changed.md) | Yes — roaster, bean and profile dependent [S0020](../sources/S0020-home-barista-bean-temperature-at-first-crack.md) [S0019](../sources/S0019-sweet-marias-first-crack-faq.md) [S0024](../sources/S0024-home-barista-first-crack-temperature-changed.md) | contested — [Measurement](measurement-and-artisan.md#contested) |
| What does "baked" mean? | RoR-crash flavour: hollow, flat, less sweet [S0008](../sources/S0008-rao-what-is-baked-coffee.md) | Very long development at equal colour: bitter, roasty [S0006](../sources/S0006-alstrup-2020-development-time-sensory.md) | definitions differ — say which one you mean |

## Not myths, but often misapplied
- **Comparing temperatures across machines.** First crack reads ~180–185 °C on the Skywalker
  [S0001](../sources/S0001-skywiki-infrared-roaster.md) [S0015](../sources/S0015-homeroasters-skywalker-roasts-thread.md) [S0017](../sources/S0017-homeroasters-skywalker-mods-thread.md) vs ~200 °C on convection machines [S0019](../sources/S0019-sweet-marias-first-crack-faq.md) [S0020](../sources/S0020-home-barista-bean-temperature-at-first-crack.md); drum-roaster numbers don't transfer 1:1 — show them as reported and adjusted, see [Temperatures](temperature-adjustment.md) and [Measurement](measurement-and-artisan.md).
- **"Development time" without context.** The same time means different things after a fast vs slow
  roast or a high vs low RoR at first crack [S0018](../sources/S0018-rao-2014-development-time-ratio.md) [S0021](../sources/S0021-home-barista-development-time-light-roasts.md). See [Development](development.md).
