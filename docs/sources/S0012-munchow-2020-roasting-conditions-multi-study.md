# S0012 — Roasting Conditions and Coffee Flavor: a multi-study investigation (Münchow et al., 2020)

**Author:** Münchow, Alstrup, Steen, Giacalone · **Type:** peer-reviewed paper · **Grade:** A · **Heat context:** multiple (mostly flame drum) · [Original](https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5710/6/2/29)

## Summary
Pools eight sensory studies (descriptive analysis; 6 with trained panels of 7–11, 2 with ~46–49
semi-trained professionals) on washed Arabicas from Kenya, Colombia and Ethiopia to compare how
much **roast colour** versus **roast time** drives flavour, splitting time into *time to first
crack* and *development time*. Colour was the stronger predictor overall. Darker and longer both
pushed toward more bitterness and less acidity, fruitiness and sweetness. At the same colour,
development time mattered more than time to first crack; total time alone wasn't correlated
with any attribute in the studies where it was checked.

## Key claims
1. **C1** — Both colour and time affect flavour, but colour (roast degree) is the stronger predictor.
   > "colour was the stronger predictor of the two." — *Abstract*
2. **C2** — Darker roasts and longer roast times push flavour the same way: more bitterness, less acidity, fruitiness and sweetness.
   > (Abstract: darker roasts/longer times "associated with an increase in bitterness and a decrease in acidity, fruitiness, and sweetness") — *Abstract*
3. **C3** — At the same colour, development time had a bigger effect on flavour than time to first crack.
   > "development time, rather than time to first crack, had the largest effect on coffee flavour." — *Abstract*
4. **C4** — Development time significantly affected all sensory attributes measured, while time to first crack affected only some.
   > "development time had a significant effect on all sensory attributes" — *Section 3.2*
5. **C5** — Practical size of the effect: ~3 min of development corresponds to about a 2 cm drop in acidity on a 10 cm scale.
   > "this would result in a decrease in acidity of approximately 2 cm on a 10 cm line scale" — *Results*
6. **C6** — Total roast time on its own was not correlated with any flavour attribute (studies 1–2).
   > (as reported in the results for studies 1–2) — *Results*
7. **C7** — Body was not affected by either time phase.
   > (as reported) — *Results*

## Relevance to Skywalker (IR, 350 g)
*Gatherer judgement:* Very relevant for priorities: first get **roast degree** right, then use
**development time** as the main fine-tuning lever; fiddling with the pre-first-crack path is
lower-yield (at equal colour). Mechanism is chemistry, so direction should transfer to IR.
**Tension with S0011**, which emphasizes pre-first-crack phase timing.
