# S0003 — Renatoa Words of Wisdom (Skywiki)

**Author:** Renatoa (quotes compiled on Skywiki) · **Type:** forum quotes · **Grade:** C · **Heat context:** IR · [Original](https://skywiki.coffee/books/general-information/page/renatoa-words-of-wisdom)

## Summary
A collection of quotes from a highly experienced Skywalker owner on how the machine works:
it does not roast with hot air — the air actually takes heat away from the beans; the bean
probe reads an air–bean mix, so readings late in the roast are lower than on convection
roasters and lower still with smaller loads; the machine is very sensitive to airflow
changes; and a heavily used lamp lasted ~9 months (~135 kg).

## Key claims
1. **C1** — The Skywalker doesn't roast with hot air at all; the air removes heat from the beans.
   > "Skywalker don't use hot air to roast beans... at all ! Conversely, the air steals heat from the beans." — *Skywalker*
2. **C2** — Any probe in a moving bean mass measures an average that includes the surrounding air; it can't accurately read bean surfaces.
   > "every known method of measuring a moving mass of solid objects measures an average of the air temperature around the solids" — *On BT*
3. **C3** — Because the air is cooler than the beans in the Skywalker, end-of-roast readings are relatively lower than on convection roasters.
   > "you will experience a relatively lower temperature for the final part of the roasting in the Skywalker" — *On BT*
4. **C4** — With smaller loads the probe sees more air relative to beans, so it reads lower.
   > "for lower loads the probe measures a mix consisting of more air than beans, thus lower figures on display." — *On Probes*
5. **C5** — Airflow changes alter roast dynamics dramatically — even a kitchen hood's speed setting ~0.5 m above the roaster was detectable.
   > "Any change in this machine airflow will change dramatically the roast dynamics, don't do this !" — *On Airflow*
6. **C6** — A heavily used machine's heater filament broke after ~9 months at ~15 kg/month (~135 kg); production users should keep a spare lamp.
   > "9 months, 15 kg per month, thus about 135 kgs." — *On Skywalker Lamps*

## Numbers & parameters

|---|---|---|---|
| Lamp life (one anecdote) | ~135 kg (9 months × 15 kg) | Skywalker, heavy use | C6 |

## Relevance to Skywalker (IR, 350 g)
*Gatherer judgement:* Directly relevant. C4 matters for comparing 350 g vs 400 g roasts — BT
readings will not be comparable across batch sizes. C5 means fan setting (and room airflow) must
be kept consistent across experiments.

## Gatherer notes
"Air steals heat" conflicts in emphasis with the Skywalker's built-in programs raising fan to 90 %
late in the roast (S0001 C6) — that may be deliberate cooling of the lamp/drum. The page frames
the content as quotes; original forum posts not linked.
