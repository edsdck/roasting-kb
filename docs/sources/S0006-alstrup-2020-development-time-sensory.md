# S0006 — Development time modulations: sensory and chemical effects (Alstrup et al., 2020)

**Author:** Alstrup, Petersen, Larsen, Münchow · **Type:** peer-reviewed paper · **Grade:** A · **Heat context:** gas-drum · [Original](https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5710/6/4/70)

## Summary
Four roasts of one washed Colombian coffee were made with the same path to first crack (~570 s)
and the same final colour (Agtron 76 ± 1), varying only development time: 90, 143, 266 and
390 s. Same colour was reached by dropping at lower temperatures for longer developments. 46
experienced tasters rated nine descriptors; NMR and GC–MS measured chemistry. Shorter
development gave sweeter, more acidic, fruitier, cleaner cups; longer development gave more
bitter, roasted, nutty-chocolate, astringent cups. Body did not change. Conclusion: colour alone
does not describe a roast — development time matters too.

## Key claims
1. **C1** — At equal colour, shorter development increases fruity, sweet and acidic characteristics.
   > "a short development time increases the fruity, sweet and acidic characteristics of the coffee" — *Abstract*
2. **C2** — At equal colour, longer development shifts the cup toward roasty, nutty and bitter.
   > "a longer development time shifts the balance towards a more roasty, nutty, and bitter profile." — *Abstract*
3. **C3** — Body was not significantly affected by development time (p = 0.37); all other attributes were (p < 0.001).
   > (Table, sensory means — Body 6.9 / 7.4 / 7.2 / 7.1, p = 0.37) — *Results, sensory table*
4. **C4** — Roast colour alone is not sufficient to characterize a roast; QC should record development time too.
   > "roast colour alone is not a sufficient indicator of the chemical and sensory properties of the coffee." — *Discussion/Conclusions*
5. **C5** — Chlorogenic acid (5-CQA) decreased with longer development (3.52 → 2.44 mM fast → baked); caffeine stayed roughly constant.
   > (NMR results; values as reported) — *Results, NMR*

## Relevance to Skywalker (IR, 350 g)
*Gatherer judgement:* The direction of the effect (short development = brighter, long = roastier
at equal colour) is chemistry-driven and likely transfers. Absolute times/temps won't: the
Skywalker reads first crack ~182 °C (S0001), so drop temperatures must be translated.

## Gatherer notes
Here "Baked" means very long development (390 s) — a different definition from Rao's
"baked = RoR crash" (S0008). One origin, one colour, light roast. Professional tasters, not
consumers. Development "% of total time" can be computed: Fast ≈ 14 %, Baked ≈ 41 %.
