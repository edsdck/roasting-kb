# S0043 — Efecto de las temperaturas y tiempos de tueste en la calidad sensorial del café (Cenicafé, 2022)

**Author:** Osorio Pérez & Pabón Usaquén · **Type:** peer-reviewed paper (Spanish; quotes below are in Spanish with an English translation) · **Grade:** A · **Heat context:** not stated · [Original](https://cenicafe.org/es/publications/Articulo_EfectoTemp.pdf)

## Summary
Cenicafé tested whether changing only the **initial (charge) temperature** of a production roaster changes cup quality when every batch is stopped at the same colour (Agtron/SCA 55–65, a medium roast). Six Colombian improved varieties were each roasted on five curves, with initial temperatures of 200 °C (base), 215 and 230 °C (higher) and 185 and 170 °C (lower). Roast time moved from about 8 to 12 min with charge temperature, but the cup scores of five Q-Graders did not respond to charge temperature; only variety mattered. For a home roaster the useful message is narrow: within this medium-roast target, a wide window of charge temperatures gave the same cup score. It does not say anything about development time, rate of rise or light roasts, and the roaster is not described.

## Key claims
1. **C1** — Five initial temperatures were compared on a base curve of 200 °C.
   > "La curva base tuvo como temperatura inicial 200°C (CB)" — *Materiales y Métodos*
   > *Translation: "The base curve had an initial temperature of 200 °C (CB)."*
   > "dos curvas con incrementos de temperatura a 215°C (I-15) y 230°C (I-30)" — *Materiales y Métodos*
   > *Translation: "two curves with temperature increases to 215 °C (I-15) and 230 °C (I-30)."*
   > "dos curvas con disminución de la temperatura de la curva base de tueste a 185 °C (D-15) y 170 °C (D-30)" — *Materiales y Métodos*
   > *Translation: "two curves with the base roasting curve's temperature decreased to 185 °C (D-15) and 170 °C (D-30)."*
2. **C2** — Every batch was stopped by colour, not by time.
   > "el tiempo final de tueste fue definido por el color del grano 55-65 en la escala AGTRON/SCA para tostado" — *Resultados y Discusión, Tiempo*
   > *Translation: "the final roasting time was defined by the bean colour 55–65 on the AGTRON/SCA scale for roasting."*
3. **C3** — Roast time ranged from 8 to 12 min; the longest average was at the lowest charge temperature and the shortest at the highest.
   > "Los tiempos de tueste oscilaron entre 8 y 12 minutos" — *Resumen*
   > *Translation: "Roasting times ranged between 8 and 12 minutes."*
   > "obteniéndose los mayores tiempos de tueste en el tratamiento D-30, con un promedio de 11,47 minutos" — *Resumen*
   > *Translation: "the longest roasting times were obtained in treatment D-30, with an average of 11.47 minutes."*
   > "y los menores se presentaron en el tratamiento I-30 con un promedio de 8,39 minutos" — *Resumen*
   > *Translation: "and the shortest occurred in treatment I-30 with an average of 8.39 minutes."*
4. **C4** — Roast time depended on the interaction of charge temperature and variety.
   > "El tiempo de tueste mostró efecto por la interacción temperatura inicial de tueste y variedad" — *Conclusiones*
   > *Translation: "Roasting time showed an effect from the interaction of initial roasting temperature and variety."*
5. **C5** — Fragrance/aroma, flavour and total score did not respond to the initial temperature; only variety had an effect.
   > "no mostraron efecto por los cambios en la temperatura inicial de tueste" — *Conclusiones*
   > *Translation: "showed no effect from the changes in the initial roasting temperature."*
6. **C6** — Design: lots of 25 kg split into 5 kg work units, one treatment per unit; the cup was scored by five certified Q-Graders.
   > "cada una de 25 kg, y cada una fue dividida en cinco unidades de trabajo de 5 kg" — *Materiales y Métodos*
   > *Translation: "each of 25 kg, and each was divided into five work units of 5 kg."*
   > "con cinco catadores certificados Q–Grader por el CQI (Coffee Quality Institute)" — *Materiales y Métodos*
   > *Translation: "with five Q-Grader tasters certified by the CQI (Coffee Quality Institute)."*
7. **C7** — Citric acid was the chemical variable most widely tied to the sensory scores (weak to moderate correlations).
   > "El ácido cítrico tuvo relación directa con la mayoría de los atributos sensoriales" — *Conclusiones*
   > *Translation: "Citric acid had a direct relationship with most of the sensory attributes."*
8. **C8** — The authors say a roast should at least control temperature, time and roast degree when green coffee quality is inconsistent.
   > "El tueste debe asegurar mínimo el control de variables como temperatura, tiempo y grado de tueste" — *Conclusiones*
   > *Translation: "roasting must at minimum ensure control of variables such as temperature, time and degree of roast."*
9. **C9** — The paper cites the literature for a weight loss of 11–20 % in roasting; it does not report weight loss for its own roasts.
   > "se produce una pérdida de peso del 11%-20%" — *opening background text*
   > *Translation: "a weight loss of 11%–20% occurs."*

## Relevance to Skywalker (IR, 350 g)
*Gatherer judgement:* A controlled, replicated test that a wide change in charge temperature (170–230 °C on a production roaster) changes roast time by about 3 min but not the cup score at a medium roast with matched colour. It supports the idea that charge temperature is a lever for roast time, and that colour at the end matters more for the cup than the path to it. Limits: unnamed roaster of a different class, 5 kg units, medium roast only, no development time, RoR or first-crack data, and colour matching means roast degree was not a variable. Treat it as weak support for "charge temperature is forgiving", not for any IR-specific setting.
