# S0037 — Gayo Arabica: development-phase length vs cup (Fadhil et al., 2025)

**Author:** Fadhil, Adam, Subekti, Agustina · **Type:** peer-reviewed paper · **Grade:** A (weak design; see notes) · **Heat context:** gas-drum · [Original](https://gup.ugal.ro/ugaljournals/index.php/food/article/download/9287/7968)

## Summary
One washed Gayo (Aceh) Arabica was roasted three times in a 2 kg gas batch roaster with drying and Maillard fixed at 4:00 each and the development phase (time after first crack) set to 2:50, 3:50 and 4:50. Total times were 10:50, 11:50 and 12:50. Two Q Graders cupped the roasts (SCA-style scores) and nine panellists ranked them. The shortest development scored highest on both counts (85 vs 82 vs 81), but it was also the lightest roast (Agtron #55 vs #45 vs #35), so time and colour are mixed up, unlike S0006 which held colour constant. One roast per treatment, no replicates.

## Key claims
1. **C1** — Design: only the development phase was changed; the paper defines it as time after first crack.
   > "the Development phase was determined based on the time interval after the first crack occurred." — *Results and discussion, Roasting chart*
2. **C2** — The shortest development (2:50, total 10:50) gave the best sensory quality; the authors recommend it for this coffee.
   > "yielded the best sensory quality in sample A1" — *Conclusions*
   > "with a Development phase duration of 2 minutes and 50 seconds" — *Conclusions*
3. **C3** — Longer development gave darker roasts (Agtron #55, #45, #35 for A1, A2, A3).
   > "The smaller the number, the darker the color of the coffee beans." — *Results and discussion, Calculating the Agtron Scale of Coffee Beans*
4. **C4** — Cup scores fell with development time: final 85.00, 82.00, 81.00; aroma, flavour, aftertaste, acidity, body and balance each scored highest for A1 (Table 2). The authors call the differences small but real.
   > "Even though the differences are small, different treatments can result in changes to the taste" — *Results and discussion, Cupping score assessment test*
5. **C5** — The cupping is thin: two certified Q Graders, one roast per treatment, no replicate statistics; the authors admit different assessors could differ.
   > "certified panelists (Q graders), namely a group of trained panelists who already have a license" — *Materials and methods, Cupping test*
   > "Even though they used the same criteria, their assessment results could be different." — *Results and discussion, after Table 8*
6. **C6** — Roaster and start: LPG burner, start at 170 °C drum temperature and 3 psi gas pressure, held constant to drop (heating was not changed between roasts).
   > "Heating is provided by an LPG burner, regulated by a thermocontrol and solenoid system." — *Materials and methods, Materials*
   > "operated at an initial drum temperature of 170 °C and a gas pressure of 3 psi." — *Materials and methods, Roasting process*
7. **C7** — Green coffee: 10.25 % moisture, 750 g/L bulk density, full-washed, from Central Aceh.
   > "with a moisture content of 10.25%, processed using the full-wash method" — *Materials and methods, Materials*

## Relevance to Skywalker (IR, 350 g)
*Gatherer judgement:* A direct but weak data point for the owner's open question about short development: at fixed heat, the shortest of three developments (2:50, about 26 % of the roast) cupped best on a commercial-size gas roaster. Nothing below 2:50 was tested, colour changed with time, and the machine and batch size are very different from a 350 g IR roaster. Best used alongside S0006 (equal colour, 90–390 s) rather than on its own.

## Gatherer notes
- Values were read from the paper through a text-summarising fetch tool (not raw page text); the quotes above were confirmed in a second fetch, table values come from the same tool and are recorded as given.
- Inconsistencies in the paper: A3 development is 4:50 in the table and "4.5 minutes" in the text; the text says first crack is about 190 °C while measured values are 192.8–196.8 °C; Figure 2's caption names A1 for the A2 chart.
- Cupping details (days of rest, grind, water) are not given. Weight loss is not reported.
- Table 2 as read: the A2 attribute scores add to 81.50, not the stated 82.00; the text says 11 attributes were assessed but the table lists 10. The turning-point time for A1 is not stated.
- Drop temperature is not monotonic with development time (213.6 / 221.6 / 219.2 °C), so the roasts differ in more than time.
