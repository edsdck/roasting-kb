# Roasting Knowledge Base

A personal, continuously updated knowledge base on coffee roasting — built from articles, papers,
videos and podcasts, plus my own roasts on a **Skywalker V1** (infrared-heated, ~350 g batches,
logged with Artisan).

It is maintained by two AI agents: a **Gatherer** that finds, vets and summarizes sources, and a
**Builder** that integrates them here, corrects or marks disagreements, and logs every change.

## Sections
- **[Knowledge base](kb/index.md)** — topic pages
- **[My Skywalker](skywalker/index.md)** — my roast log, findings and next experiments
- **[Sources](sources/index.md)** — every source with summary, claims and quotes
- **[Changelog](changelog.md)** — what changed and why

## How to read it
| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `conf: high` | Two or more independent strong sources agree |
| `conf: medium` | One strong source, or several practitioners agree |
| `conf: low` | Single opinion or weak source |
| `conf: contested` | Credible sources disagree — both sides shown |
| `ctx: gas-drum` etc. | Evidence comes from a different heat source than my IR roaster — translate with care |
| **Inference — test it** | Builder's reasoning, not a sourced fact |

Source grades: **A** experiment / paper · **B** practitioner with data · **C** practitioner
opinion · **D** marketing / unverified · **P** my own roasts.
